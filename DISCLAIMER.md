# GiriDrishti AI — Disclaimer

## 1. General Disclaimer

GiriDrishti AI is an academic and research prototype developed for the purpose of demonstrating an AI-assisted approach to landslide risk monitoring.

The system generates estimates based on available input data and machine-learning models.

The results produced by the system are not guaranteed predictions of future landslide events.

---

## 2. Not an Official Warning System

GiriDrishti AI does not constitute:

* An official government warning system
* An emergency notification authority
* A geological certification system
* A professional geological assessment
* A substitute for disaster-management authorities
* A substitute for emergency services
* A guarantee that a landslide will or will not occur

Risk classifications displayed by the application are intended for research, demonstration, monitoring, and decision-support purposes.

---

## 3. Safety-Critical Decisions

Users must not rely solely on GiriDrishti AI for safety-critical decisions.

For emergency or evacuation decisions, users should follow information and instructions issued by appropriate government authorities, disaster-management agencies, local administration, and qualified professionals.

---

## 4. Machine Learning Limitations

Machine-learning predictions depend on:

* Data quality
* Dataset coverage
* Input accuracy
* Feature selection
* Model assumptions
* Training data
* Environmental conditions

Actual landslide behavior may differ from model estimates.

The system may produce:

* False positives
* False negatives
* Incorrect classifications
* Uncertain predictions

---

## 5. Historical Data

Historical landslide information used by the project is not owned by the project team where it originates from external sources.

The original source should be consulted for authoritative information.

Historical occurrence does not guarantee future occurrence.

---

## 6. Environmental Conditions

Landslides can be affected by complex factors that may not be represented by the current prototype, including:

* Geological structure
* Rock type
* Groundwater conditions
* Drainage
* Land use
* Construction activity
* Vegetation
* Seismic activity
* Extreme weather
* Human intervention

---

## 7. No Guarantee

The project team does not guarantee:
